\begin{abstract}
Held-out respondents reveal only their always-visible \textsc{given} block, so every scored cell is a draw from $p(\text{answer}\mid\textsc{given})$, gate sentinel included. We fit this conditional distribution entirely inside \texttt{predict()} on the visible rows, with no pretrained model and no external data. Four candidate predictors -- the crowd marginal, a Dirichlet count backoff, multi-task softmax regression and three multi-task MLPs with one softmax head per item -- produce out-of-fold probabilities by 5-fold cross-validation; a per-item convex mixture, shrunk towards a shared mixture, is fitted to the out-of-fold log score with the grader's probability floor applied. Skip-logic rules that hold without exception on the visible rows are then imposed exactly. Every choice that could overfit is made by cross-validation on the delivered data rather than tuned on the synthetic sandbox. Test-phase skill: \todo{test score}.
\end{abstract}

\noindent Supervised prediction: multi-task softmax regression and MLPs. Hierarchical: Dirichlet backoff from the marginal to one- and two-column strata. Stacking: per-item convex mixture fitted on out-of-fold log score. Rules: gate sentinels decided by a visible parent.

\subsection{Pipeline}
\label{sec:pipeline}
For each instrument, \texttt{predict(frame, schema)} (i) encodes every \textsc{given} column one-hot (the gate sentinel is its own level); (ii) splits the visible rows into $F{=}5$ folds; (iii) on each fold fits M1--M5 below on the other folds and predicts the held-out fold and all hidden rows; (iv) fits the stacking weights on the out-of-fold probabilities; (v) averages the $F$ fold models' hidden-row predictions for the networks, refits M0/M1 on all visible rows, and mixes; (vi) imposes verified gate rules; (vii) returns one vector per blank cell in canonical order (rows top to bottom, items in schema order). The count backoff is the one in the organizers' \texttt{external\_model} baseline with a uniform base measure~\citep{sbstarter}.

\subsection{Inputs and feature construction}
Only the \textsc{given} block is used, because it is the only information visible for a held-out respondent; visible respondents' \textsc{predict} answers are used as training labels. Question wording is not used (see Limitations). Each \textsc{predict} item's label space is its option list plus the gate sentinel when the item is gated, exactly the scored vector.

\subsection{Model or simulator}
\textbf{M0} crowd marginal (counts $+\tfrac12$). \textbf{M1} Dirichlet backoff: marginal $\to$ stratum of the most informative \textsc{given} column $\to$ the two most informative columns (plug-in mutual information), $\alpha{=}2$ pseudo-counts per level. \textbf{M2} multi-task softmax regression: one linear layer from the one-hot \textsc{given} vector to all items' logits, padded to the widest item and masked. \textbf{M3--M5} multi-task MLPs sharing hidden layers across all items of an instrument: $[256,256]$ GELU, dropout 0.3; $[512]$, dropout 0.5; $[256,128,128]$, dropout 0.2. Loss: mean over items of the categorical cross-entropy. Items are treated as conditionally independent given \textsc{given}, which is exact for the metric: every cell is scored by its own marginal log score.

\subsection{Fitting, training, or prompting}
All fitting happens at prediction time on the visible rows of the phase (\textsc{train} in development, \textsc{train}+\textsc{dev} in the final phase). Networks: AdamW (lr $10^{-2}$ linear, $1$--$2\times10^{-3}$ MLPs, weight decay $10^{-4}$--$10^{-3}$), minibatch 256--512, up to 60--80 epochs; \emph{per item}, the epoch with the lowest validation loss on the fold is kept (patience 6--8 epochs without any item improving). Seeds are fixed.

\subsection{Prediction and aggregation}
With $P_m(i,j)$ the out-of-fold probability model $m$ gives respondent $i$'s true answer to item $j$, the mixture weights $w_j=\mathrm{softmax}(b+a_j)$ maximise
$\frac1{NJ}\sum_{i,j}\log\big(\varepsilon+(1-K_j\varepsilon)\sum_m w_{jm}P_m(i,j)\big)-\lambda\,\overline{a^2}$, $\varepsilon{=}10^{-3}$, $\lambda{=}0.05$ (400 Adam steps), so items with little evidence fall back on the shared mixture $b$.

\subsection{Post-processing and calibration (if applicable)}
For gated items whose gate is decided by visible data (a \textsc{given} parent, or a chain closed by one), the rule ``closed $\Rightarrow$ sentinel, open $\Rightarrow$ not sentinel'' is imposed only if it holds for every visible respondent (and at least 20 are covered). Calibration towards the crowd is the stacking weight on M0; the grader's own floor mixing is applied after.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
The method only knows what the \textsc{given} block reveals; it cannot learn anything about subgroups that are rare in the visible rows beyond what the shared hidden layers and the shrinkage to the crowd provide. It ignores question wording, so it cannot improve on rare answer options from world knowledge. Sandbox scores are not evidence about the real instruments.\label{end-of-main}

\section{Full configuration}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Folds $F{=}5$, fold seed 0; network seeds $17f$ for fold $f$.
  \item M2: no hidden layer, lr $10^{-2}$, wd $10^{-4}$, batch 512, $\le$60 epochs, patience 6.
  \item M3: $[256,256]$, dropout 0.3, lr $2\times10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-4}$, batch 256, $\le$60 epochs, patience 6.
  \item M4: $[512]$, dropout 0.5, lr $2\times10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-3}$, batch 256, $\le$60 epochs, patience 6.
  \item M5: $[256,128,128]$, dropout 0.2, lr $10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-4}$, batch 256, $\le$80 epochs, patience 8.
  \item Stacking: $\lambda{=}0.05$, Adam lr 0.05, 400 steps, float64.
  \item Time guard: if a call exceeds 240\,s (or 650\,s since import), remaining fold models are skipped.
\end{itemize}
